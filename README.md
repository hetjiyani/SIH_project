# 🌾 AgriSmart AI — Your Smart Farm Assistant & Autopilot

> **An easy-to-use digital farm companion that helps farmers protect crops, save water, cut electricity bills, and grow healthier harvests.**

---

## 💡 What is AgriSmart AI in Simple Words?

Farming is hard work. Farmers have to make difficult decisions every single day:
* *“Is my tomato leaf sick with a dangerous fungus, or is it just dry?”*
* *“Should I turn on the water pump today, or will it rain tonight and flood my field?”*
* *“Is today safe to spray pest medicine, or will the wind blow it away?”*
* *“What crop should I plant in my soil this season to earn the best profit?”*

**AgriSmart AI** is like having an **expert crop doctor, a weather scientist, and an automatic farm manager in your pocket 24/7**. 

You don't need any technical skills to use it. You can take a picture of a leaf, check simple YES/NO water advice, ask questions in your own language (like Hindi or Gujarati), or let the AI automatically manage your water valves.

---

## 🗺️ What Does Each Page Do? (A-to-Z Simple Guide)

AgriSmart AI is divided into **7 simple tools**. Here is what each one does:

---

### 🌿 Page 1: Crop Disease Doctor (Find & Cure Plant Sickness)
* **What problem it solves:** When leaves get spots, farmers often don't know what disease it is. They either buy the wrong expensive chemicals or wait too long until the crop dies.
* **How it works:**
  1. Take a photo of the sick leaf with your phone (or pick one of our sample leaves).
  2. The AI scans the leaf in 2 seconds.
  3. It tells you the exact disease name, how sure it is, and whether the plant is in danger.
* **What you get:**
  * **Natural / Organic Remedies:** Home recipes and organic bio-sprays (like Neem oil or natural bio-fungicides) that don't harm the soil.
  * **Farm Medicine:** The exact recommended medicine and dose if you need a chemical spray.
  * **How to stop it next time:** Simple farming habits to prevent the fungus from coming back.
  * 🔊 **Audio Readout:** A button that reads the advice out loud so anyone can listen without reading small text.

---

### 🌾 Page 2: Smart Crop Picker (What Should I Grow?)
* **What problem it solves:** Planting the wrong crop in the wrong soil or season can lead to total crop failure and heavy financial loss.
* **How it works:**
  1. Choose your region (e.g. Gujarat, Maharashtra, Punjab, etc.) or let the app detect where you are.
  2. Enter your soil type (like black soil, clay, or sandy) and pH if you know it.
* **What you get:**
  * A ranked list of the best crops to plant right now (like Cotton, Wheat, Mustard, Groundnut, or Tomato).
  * It tells you how much water each crop needs, how many months it takes to harvest, and how much profit you can expect.

---

### 💧 Page 3: Smart Irrigation (Should I Water Today?)
* **What problem it solves:** Over-watering drowns plant roots and wastes thousands of liters of groundwater. Under-watering dries out the plants.
* **How it works:**
  * The app looks at how damp your soil is and checks satellite rain forecasts for the next 24 hours.
* **What you get:**
  * A big, clear answer: **NO — Irrigation Delay Advised** or **YES — Water Field Now**.
  * If rain is coming tonight, it warns you: *“Don't turn on your pump! Rain will water your field tonight for free and save you 15,000 liters of water.”*
  * **Interactive Test Simulator:** You can slide rainfall and soil sliders to see for yourself how the AI changes its decision when rain approaches.

---

### 🌤️ Page 4: 7-Day Weather & Spray Planner
* **What problem it solves:** If a farmer sprays expensive pesticide and it rains 2 hours later, all the medicine washes away into the dirt. If it's too windy, the spray blows into the neighbor's field.
* **How it works:**
  * Checks live weather right over your village or farm coordinates.
* **What you get:**
  * A 7-day temperature and rain forecast.
  * **Green / Red Spray Badges:**
    * 🟢 *Safe to Spray:* Calm wind and zero rain — your medicine will stick to the leaves.
    * 🔴 *High Wind Warning:* Wind is too strong — don't spray today or you will waste your money.
    * 🌧️ *Rain Wash-Off Warning:* Rain coming soon — delay spraying.

---

### 🌱 Page 5: Farm Sustainability Scorecard (Farm Health Grade)
* **What problem it solves:** Helps farmers understand if they are using too much chemical fertilizer or wasting water, and how to make their farm healthier for years to come.
* **How it works:**
  * Like a report card for your farm, from **0 to 100 points** with letter grades (**Grade A, B, C, D, or F**).
* **What you get:**
  * **Interactive Practice Sliders:** Move the sliders for weekly water, fertilizer bags, or irrigation type (Drip vs. Flood).
  * Watch your grade change in real time:
    * Using Drip Irrigation + moderate fertilizer $\rightarrow$ **Grade A (92/100)** 🏆
    * Flooding the field + heavy chemicals $\rightarrow$ **Grade D (45/100)** ⚠️
  * Gives you 3 simple tips on how to save money on fertilizer and boost your score.

---

### 🗣️ Page 6: Farm AI Helper (Ask Any Question in Your Language)
* **What problem it solves:** Farmers often have questions late at night or when agricultural officers are not available.
* **How it works:**
  * A friendly chat assistant that speaks **10 Indian languages** (English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, and Odia).
  * You can type or ask questions like:
    * *“Why are the edges of my potato leaves turning black?”*
    * *“How can I make natural fertilizer at home?”*
* **Why it's smart:**
  * It knows what crop you have and what your farm's weather is right now, so its answers are tailored to your real field, not generic textbook answers.

---

### ⚡ Page 7: Farm Autopilot (The Automatic Farm Manager)
* **What problem it solves:** Farmers cannot be awake 24 hours a day watching every water valve, solar pump, and weather alert.
* **What it does:**
  * This is the **Autopilot of the farm**. It runs in the background every 30 minutes without you touching anything.
  * **Automatic Actions It Takes:**
    * 🌧️ **Automatic Rain Shutoff:** When it detects rain coming, it turns off smart water valves so you don't over-water.
    * ☀️ **Free Solar Pumping:** When the midday sun is brightest, it runs the solar water pump so watering costs **0 ₹** in electric bills.
    * 🍄 **Fungus Alert:** If the air stays warm and humid for 6 hours, it alerts you that mold spores are about to hatch before you can even see them.
    * ❄️ **Heatwave Protection:** If a 42°C heatwave hits, it sends a 15-minute fine mist to cool the plant leaves so flowers don't drop off.
  * **The Interactive Sandbox (Top of Page):**
    * Let anyone test how the AI works! Click buttons like **Monsoon Surge** or **Fungal Blight Risk** to see what sensors detect, why the AI makes its choice, and which physical machine (valve or pump) it turns on or off!
  * **The Live Activity Log (Bottom of Page):**
    * A list of everything the AI did while you were away, so you always stay in control.

## 💻 Complete Local Setup Guide (Connect Backend, Frontend & ML Model)

Anyone can run **AgriSmart AI** locally on their laptop (macOS, Windows, or Linux) in under 5 minutes.

### 🏗️ How Backend, Frontend & ML Model Connect

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   Web Browser UI                       │
│        (frontend/index.html & frontend/js/api.js)      │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │ REST API Requests (/api/*)
                            ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               FastAPI Backend Server                   │
│                (backend/main.py :8000)                 │
│  - Serves REST API endpoints (/api/*, /predict, etc.)  │
│  - Hosts Frontend Web UI statically at /frontend       │
│  - Handles CORS for cross-origin local testing         │
└─────────────┬───────────────────────────┬──────────────┘
              │ Loads on boot             │ Queries for live data
              ▼                           ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐  ┌─────────────────────────┐
│  PyTorch Deep Learning    │  │ External Integrations   │
│  EfficientNet-B0 Model    │  │ - Open-Meteo (Weather)  │
│ (model/best_agri_model.pth│  │ - Google Gemini (GenAI) │
│   38 Pathology Classes)   │  └─────────────────────────┘
└───────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Frontend**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript located in `frontend/`. It communicates with the backend via `frontend/js/api.js` pointing to `/api`.
2. **Backend**: A high-performance Python FastAPI server in `backend/` that handles leaf validation guardrails, crop recommendations, irrigation calculations, and agentic workflows. It also mounts and serves the frontend directly.
3. **ML Model**: A trained PyTorch EfficientNet-B0 weights file located at `model/best_agri_model.pth` (16.5 MB, trained on 38 PlantVillage pathology classes). On startup, `backend/services/disease_service.py` automatically detects and loads these weights on CPU (or CUDA GPU if available).

### 📋 Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure your laptop has:
* **Python 3.10, 3.11, or 3.12** installed ([Download Python](https://www.python.org/downloads/))
* **Git** installed ([Download Git](https://git-scm.com/downloads))
* Any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Safari)

Verify your Python version in terminal/command prompt:
```bash
python3 --version   # or: python --version
```

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Installation

#### Step 1: Clone the Repository
Open your Terminal (macOS/Linux) or Command Prompt / PowerShell (Windows) and clone the repository:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/ManavVora26/AgriSmart-AI.git
cd AgriSmart-AI
```

#### Step 2: Create and Activate a Virtual Environment

* **On macOS / Linux:**
  ```bash
  python3 -m venv venv
  source venv/bin/activate
  ```

* **On Windows (Command Prompt):**
  ```cmd
  python -m venv venv
  venv\Scripts\activate.bat
  ```

* **On Windows (PowerShell):**
  ```powershell
  python -m venv venv
  venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
  ```
  *(Note: If PowerShell shows an execution policy error, run `Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process -ExecutionPolicy Bypass` first).*

#### Step 3: Install Required Dependencies
Install the necessary Python packages (FastAPI, PyTorch, Torchvision, Scikit-learn, etc.):
```bash
pip install --upgrade pip
pip install -r backend/requirements.txt
```

#### Step 4: Configure Environment & Verify the Model Weights

The pre-trained model weights are **already included** in this repository under `model/best_agri_model.pth`.

Create your local `.env` configuration file from the template:

* **On macOS / Linux:**
  ```bash
  cp backend/.env.example backend/.env
  ```
* **On Windows:**
  ```cmd
  copy backend\.env.example backend\.env
  ```

Open `backend/.env` in any text editor and review the settings:
```ini
# Google Gemini API Key (Optional: for AI Chat Assistant & Vision Guardrail)
# Get a free key at https://aistudio.google.com
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here

# Weather API (Open-Meteo — free, no key needed)
WEATHER_API_BASE=https://api.open-meteo.com/v1

# Path to the trained ML model weights (relative to backend/)
MODEL_WEIGHTS_PATH=../model/best_agri_model.pth

# Model mode: "real" to run PyTorch inference, or "mock" for lightweight testing
MODEL_MODE=real

# Server port and host
HOST=0.0.0.0
PORT=8000
```
> [!TIP]
> `MODEL_MODE=real` works out of the box on laptops with CPU or GPU. If you don't have an internet connection or want instant stub testing without loading the weights, you can set `MODEL_MODE=mock`.

#### Step 5: Start the AgriSmart AI Server

Navigate to the `backend` folder and start the server:
```bash
cd backend
uvicorn main:app --reload --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000
```
*(Alternatively, you can run directly from the repository root: `uvicorn backend.main:app --reload --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000`).*

When started, your terminal will display:
```
INFO:     Mounted static frontend from .../frontend at /frontend
INFO:     Real model successfully loaded from .../model/best_agri_model.pth (38 classes).
INFO:     Agentic advisor scheduler started (every 30 minutes).
INFO:     Uvicorn running on http://0.0.0.0:8000
```

#### Step 6: Open the Application

Open your browser and visit:
👉 **[http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)**

* The server automatically redirects you to the interactive frontend dashboard (`http://localhost:8000/frontend/index.html`).
* The frontend is already pre-configured to communicate with the backend API at `http://localhost:8000/api`.

### 🛠️ Optional: Running Frontend on a Separate Dev Server

If you are developing frontend features and prefer using a separate static server (such as VS Code *Live Server* or Python's HTTP server):
1. Keep the backend running on `http://localhost:8000`.
2. In a second terminal, start a frontend server:
   ```bash
   python3 -m http.server 3000 --directory frontend
   ```
3. Open `http://localhost:3000/index.html` in your browser.
4. Because CORS is enabled on the backend (`allow_origins=["*"]`), the frontend will communicate with the backend API at `http://localhost:8000/api` without any cross-origin errors.

### ❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs

* **Issue: `Address already in use` or Port 8000 is occupied**
  * Run the server on a different port:
    ```bash
    uvicorn backend.main:app --reload --port 8001
    ```
    Then access `http://localhost:8001`.
* **Issue: `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'routers'`**
  * When running from the repository root, ensure your branch contains the latest `backend/main.py` which automatically sets `sys.path`.
  * Alternatively, navigate into `backend/` and run directly:
    ```bash
    cd backend
    uvicorn main:app --reload --port 8000
    ```
* **Issue: `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'torch'` (or similar)**
  * Ensure your virtual environment is activated (`source venv/bin/activate` or `venv\Scripts\activate`) before running `pip install -r backend/requirements.txt` and `uvicorn`.
* **Issue: Leaf model running slowly on older laptops**
  * The PyTorch model runs on CPU with lightweight tensor operations. For instant response times on resource-constrained devices, you can switch `.env` to `MODEL_MODE=mock`.
* **Issue: Gemini AI Chat Assistant shows "service unavailable"**
  * Check that you added your free `GEMINI_API_KEY` into `backend/.env`. All other modules (disease detection, irrigation, crop picker, sustainability, weather) function 100% offline without any API keys.
